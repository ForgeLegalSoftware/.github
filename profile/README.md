## Tredios

### Documents In. Intelligence Out.

**Tredios** is desktop software designed for legal professionals working with case documents, email evidence, attachments, and related case data.

Tredios brings information together into an organized workspace, helping turn collections of case material into information that is easier to review, understand, and work with.
